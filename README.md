🛡️ CareGuard — NDIS & Guardianship SaaS CRM (Queensland Edition) 🇦🇺💜
A zero-server, offline-ready, single-file Guardianship & NDIS Care Organiser built for managing complex disability, neurological, mental health, and mobility support under the Queensland Public Trustee & NDIS.
🚨 Why CareGuard Exists: Safety First, Always
CareGuard was built around a real-world urgent care priority: protecting a loved one who relies on a wheelchair and lives with Schizophrenia, Functional Neurological Disorder (FND), Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID), Obesity, and Seizures.
After a critical family dinner incident—where a seizure occurred while seated in a wheelchair with a dangerously loose seatbelt—CareGuard puts immediate safety escalation, Occupational Therapy (OT) seating/restraint reviews, NDIS Plan Reassessment, Support Coordination (Coordination DD) syncing, and Public Trustee financial tracking into one calm, 1-click command center.

🚀 Quick Start Guide
Unzip & Open: Extract careguard_ndis_crm_code.zip and double-click careguard_ndis_crm.html to open it in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
Install on Phone or Desktop: Click 📱 Install App in the top bar for 1-click download (CareGuard-App.html) or Add-to-Home-Screen steps.
Set Mum's Active Plan Categories: Go to ⚙️ Settings (NDIS Categories) and flip the On/Off switches to match Mum's exact NDIS Plan.
Update Profile & Budget: Click Profile to enter Mum's NDIS number and review date, then set her category allocations in NDIS Plan & Budget.
Sync with Coordination DD: Open 🔄 Coordinator Sync Hub, click Copy Coordinator Sync Email, and import their CSV or pasted table anytime.
Back Up Regularly: Click Backup in the top bar to save a .json snapshot of all invoices, incidents, bookmarks, and care logs.
