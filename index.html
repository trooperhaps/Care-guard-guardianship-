Wheelchair & Seizure Safety Protocol

  • Pre-Outing Seatbelt Check: Verify pelvic lap belt is snug (two-finger gap max) and chest/thoracic harness is secured before any transport or dining outing.
  • During a Seizure in Wheelchair: Lock wheelchair brakes immediately, keep seatbelt fastened to prevent falling forward, cushion head, time the seizure, and do not restrain limbs.
  • Call 000 Immediately If: Seizure lasts >5 minutes, breathing difficulty occurs, head injury/fall happens, or repeated seizures occur.

FND, Schizophrenia & DID Care Notes

  • Calm Environment: Reduce sensory overload (bright lights, loud noise) when FND functional seizures or dissociative switches occur.
  • Consistent Grounding: Speak calmly, use simple grounding statements ("You are safe, we are with family"), and avoid arguing with distressing beliefs.
  • QLD 24/7 Crisis Line: Call 1300 MH CALL (1300 642 255) for mental health triage.

Weight, Mobility & Health Recovery

  • NDIS Dietitian & Exercise Physiology: Use Capacity Building — Improved Health & Wellbeing funding for wheelchair-adapted nutrition and gentle movement.
  • Pressure Care & Transfers: Review bariatric wheelchair cushion & hoist/transfer plan with Physiotherapist and OT.
